## Ground transport reimbursement
In this fictional Zava training policy, public transport is the default for an onboarding airport transfer when it is practical and operating for the journey.

A taxi or rideshare may be submitted for reimbursement when public transport is not operating for the arrival, cannot meet a practical accessibility need, or would create a documented safety concern. A preference for a taxi alone is not sufficient under this scenario policy.

Keep an itemized receipt showing the date, route and amount, and explain the qualifying reason. Submit the claim within 30 calendar days of the trip. Finance reviews the claim and manager approval; reimbursement is not automatic.
