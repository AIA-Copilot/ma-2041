## Hotel planning
This fictional Zava scenario does not contain an approved-hotel list, negotiated rates, room availability or a booking system. Do not describe any real hotel as Zava-approved.

Before requesting accommodation, provide the travel dates, number of nights, confirmed onboarding destination and practical accessibility requirements to the onboarding coordinator. The coordinator must confirm the lodging budget and approved options before a booking is made. No nightly spending limit is supplied in this dataset.

A traveler may compare options but cannot treat an agent recommendation as authorization to book or a reimbursement guarantee. Questions about the fictional process belong to travel@example.com, a reserved illustrative address that must not be contacted.
